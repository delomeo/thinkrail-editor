# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in the ThinkRail community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We judge each other by our conduct. We will not judge people by characteristics outside our control, and
we will not make these characteristics the basis of our judgment of ability.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating kindness and respect toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Owning mistakes and apologizing where our actions affected others.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as an email or phone number, without permission.
- Non-business-like comments toward an open-source project or its participants.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and chat —
and in public spaces when an individual represents the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards and take appropriate and
fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. Reports of unacceptable behavior, or requests to report an incident privately, go to the project
maintainer through a GitHub issue or the [Discord server](https://thinkrail.ai/discord). Every report is
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and
security of the reporter.
